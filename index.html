About TCC

Thimmaraju Coaching Centre in Mysore offers specialized, long-term training for K-CET, JEE, and NEET (2025-26) featuring high-profile faculty from Bangalore, Mangalore, and Mysore. They provide comprehensive study materials, scientific teaching methods, and short tricks for competitive exams. Classes include Unit-wise and Full Syllabus tests for CET/NEET preparation.
